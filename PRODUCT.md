# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Serious retail investors (and aspiring ones) who research a company before buying it. They arrive curious about a ticker, often from noise-heavy sources (Reddit, X, YouTube hype), and want a calm, evidence-driven answer to one question: "Should I own this company?" Context: desktop-first evening research sessions; dark rooms, focused reading.

## Product Purpose

Stonky is an equity research engine. The user searches a ticker and leaves with a thesis: a 0-100 score with receipts (metrics, valuation context, moat, catalysts, risks), a long-term thesis bias, and a cold, honest verdict. The landing page's job is to make the visitor feel the depth of the research system and trust it enough to run their first ticker. Core promise: "Search a ticker. Leave with a thesis."

## Brand Personality

Premium, cinematic, evidence-driven. An equity research desk with Apple/Linear-level polish: dark, editorial, confident typography; Wall Street imagery used like a film poster, not a stock-photo grid. Cold takes, receipts, conviction. The mascot (Ticky) is the friendly guide on the Ask Ticky page; the landing page stays editorial, with one "Ask Ticky Anything" moment as the bridge.

## Anti-references

- Crypto dashboards (neon, particles everywhere, fake live numbers)
- Generic AI SaaS ("unlock insights", "empower investors", gradient-text heroes)
- Stock screener templates (dense tables, feature grids, identical card rows)
- Flat section-by-section scroll pages; anything that reads "template"

## Design Principles

1. **Every score has receipts.** Show evidence, not claims; the page itself should demonstrate the research process.
2. **Travel, don't scroll.** The page is a continuous descent into the research system: pinned scenes, evolving backgrounds, layered depth.
3. **Editorial confidence.** Large, deliberate typography; imagery treated like film posters; whitespace as emphasis.
4. **Cold takes, warm craft.** Copy is blunt and anti-hype; motion is soft, expensive, 60fps.
5. **One system, one identity.** The landing page shares the app's dark grid + emerald language so the product feels continuous with its marketing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere (crossfades instead of pinned/parallax storytelling).
- Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast on dark surfaces; large display text ≥3:1.
- Touch devices get a non-pinned, simplified flow; custom cursor disabled on touch.
- Keyboard-reachable nav and CTAs.
